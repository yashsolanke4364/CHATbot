01 — Overview

Why a chatbot for student information?

Students lose hours in queues and inboxes waiting for answers that already sit in the college database. The bot puts that data one sentence away.

⏳ The problem

  • Office desks handle the same repeated queries all day.
  • Notices get lost across groups, boards and mails.
  • No help outside working hours or on holidays.
  • First-year students do not know whom to ask.

⚡ Our solution

  • An NLP engine maps a free-text question to an intent.
  • The matching record is fetched from the student database.
  • A reply lands in seconds, on any device, at any hour.
  • Unknown queries are escalated to the department desk.

03 — Features

What it can do

Hover a card to flip it.

🎓

Academic records

Marks, CGPA, backlogs

Pulls semester-wise marks, internal assessment scores and CGPA straight from the results table and formats them as a readable summary.

📅

Attendance tracker

Subject-wise percentage

Shows subject-wise attendance, warns when a student falls below the 75% detention line and counts the lectures still needed.

💳

Fees & scholarship

Dues and deadlines

Reports paid and pending fees, due dates, receipt numbers and the status of a scholarship application.

🗓

Timetable & exams

Daily and exam plans

Answers what the next lecture is, lists the day timetable and returns the published examination schedule with venues.

📚

Library desk

Issued books, fines

Lists issued books with return dates, calculates overdue fines and checks whether a title is on the shelf.

🧭

Campus guide

Faculty, departments

Finds faculty cabins and contacts, department details, hostel and transport rules, and the admission procedure.

04 — Architecture

How a question becomes an answer

Five layers, one round trip.

01

User interface

The web chat widget captures the query and renders the reply.

02

Pre-processing

Tokenise, lowercase, drop stop words, lemmatise, build a bag of words.

03

Intent classification

The trained model scores the query against every known intent.

04

Entities & database

Roll number, subject or date are extracted and the records are queried.

05

Response generation

The response template is filled and sent back to the chat window.
